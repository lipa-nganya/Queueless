Customers and vendors

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your Queueless account here or in the app. Deletion takes effect immediately and can't be undone.

In the app

  • Queueless (customers): Profile → Delete account.
  • Queueless Vendor: Settings → Delete vendor account.

What gets deleted

  • Customer accounts: your name, phone number, PIN, saved accessibility needs, queue places and bookings.
  • Vendor accounts: your account, phone number, PIN and notification devices, plus every business you manage with its branches, services, photos, bookings and queues. A business you share with another vendor stays with them.

Deletion removes this data from our live systems straight away.
