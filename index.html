About us

We're giving people back their time.

Queueless Solutions is a Kenyan software company building a digital service management platform. Waiting at barbershops, salons, clinics, banks and offices is treated as normal, even when nobody knows how long it will take or where they stand in line.

We help service businesses move from crowded waiting areas and manual queue management to organised, predictable, customer-friendly service. We help customers reach services without unnecessary waiting.

Our goal is simple: help businesses serve customers faster, operate smarter and create better experiences.

For businesses

Track staff availability and service capacity, and monitor customer flow and performance.

For customers

Better visibility on your place in line and your expected wait.

Our promise

Honest tools, no inflated claims. Wait times depend on each provider's demand and staffing.
