# Play Store listing text

## Queueless (customer app)

### Short description

Skip the line in Kenya. Join queues and book visits right from your phone.

### Full description

Stop standing in line. Queueless lets you find a place, see how busy it is, and join the queue from your phone, so you can wait wherever you like and arrive just in time.

DISCOVER PLACES NEAR YOU
Search businesses or areas and browse by category, from beauty and wellness to financial and professional services. See the current wait before you go.

JOIN THE QUEUE REMOTELY
Pick a branch and service and take your place in the queue. Your position and estimated wait update automatically, and you'll know when you're next.

BOOK AHEAD
Reserve a time that suits you. When you arrive, check in from the app to join the live queue. Plans changed? Cancel in a tap.

SHARE YOUR ACCESSIBILITY NEEDS
Save your needs once, such as step-free access, a seat while waiting, a quiet space, sign language or an assistance animal, and they're shared with the business each time you join a queue.

BROWSE WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Look around before you sign up. Log in with your phone number when you're ready to join a queue or book.

Queueless: skip the wait, across Kenya.

## Queueless Vendor (business app)

### Short description

Manage your live queue, services and branches from one simple app.

### Full description

Queueless Vendor helps businesses across Kenya serve customers without crowded waiting areas. Customers join your queue or book from their phones; you manage everything from yours.

SEE EVERY BRANCH AT A GLANCE
View all your locations in one place, with how many customers are waiting at each. Pick a branch to manage its live queue.

RUN YOUR LIVE QUEUE
Serve customers in order, mark no-shows, keep the line moving, and see any accessibility needs a customer has shared before they reach the counter.

MANAGE SERVICES PER BRANCH
Add, edit or remove services, set how long each one takes, and switch them on or off as you need.

ADD AND EDIT BRANCHES
Grow to new locations in seconds. Set each branch's address, contact number and opening hours.

SHOW WHAT YOU OFFER
Tell customers which accessibility options each branch provides, such as wheelchair access or support for blind and low-vision customers, so they can choose with confidence.

Fewer crowds, happier customers. Get started with Queueless Vendor.
